Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 12, 2026  ·  Last Updated: May 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how The Kalman Co. ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use PostKraft ("Service"). We take your privacy seriously and are committed to handling your data responsibly.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information
When you register, we collect your email address and a hashed password. We do not store your password in plain text.

Social Media OAuth Tokens
When you connect a social media account (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Pinterest, etc.), we store the OAuth access tokens provided by those platforms. These tokens are used solely to post content on your behalf and are never shared with third parties beyond the platform that issued them.

Content You Create
Photos you upload, captions you write or generate, and posts you schedule are stored temporarily to facilitate the Service. Draft posts are stored locally on your device; scheduled posts are stored on our servers until they are published or deleted.

Billing Information
Payment processing is handled by Stripe. We do not store your credit card number or full payment details. We receive and store a Stripe customer ID, subscription status, and transaction records.

Usage Data
We may collect basic usage information such as feature interactions and error logs to improve the Service. This data is not linked to identifiable individuals unless necessary to diagnose a specific issue.

2. How We Use Your Information

Purpose Data Used
Provide and operate the ServiceAccount info, OAuth tokens, content drafts
Publish posts to connected platformsOAuth tokens, post content, scheduled times
Generate AI caption suggestionsPhotos and profile context (sent to Anthropic API)
Process subscription billingEmail, Stripe customer ID

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for advertising.

3. AI Caption Generation

When you use PostKraft's AI caption feature, photos and profile context (such as your name, brokerage, and website) are transmitted to Anthropic's Claude API to generate caption suggestions. This data is subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy. We do not share your photos with any other AI provider.

4. Third-Party Services

PostKraft integrates with the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy governing their use of your data:

  • Meta (Instagram, Facebook) — Meta Privacy Policy
  • LinkedIn — LinkedIn Privacy Policy
  • Twitter / X — X Privacy Policy
  • TikTok — TikTok Privacy Policy
  • Pinterest — Pinterest Privacy Policy
  • Stripe — Stripe Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — Anthropic Privacy Policy
  • Cloudinary (image hosting for Instagram) — Cloudinary Privacy Policy

5. Data Retention

We retain your account information and OAuth tokens for as long as your account is active. Draft and scheduled post data is retained until you delete it or cancel your account. Upon account deletion, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), hashed passwords (bcrypt), and access controls on our backend infrastructure. No system is 100% secure; while we work hard to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing where applicable
  • Export your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at sean@thekalmanco.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

PostKraft is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date above and notify you by email if the changes are material. Your continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
