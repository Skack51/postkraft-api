PostKraft / Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 6, 2026  ·  Last Updated: November 6, 2026

PostKraft is a desktop app made and used by The Kalman Co. ("we," "us," or "our"), a real estate company in Nantucket, Massachusetts, to publish our own photos and posts to our own social media accounts on Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X and Pinterest. It runs on a single computer we own. It has no user accounts, no server and no billing, and it is not offered to the public. This policy explains what PostKraft collects, how it uses and stores that information, and how it is deleted.

1. What PostKraft Collects

When we connect one of our accounts, the platform gives PostKraft an access token (and, where the platform offers one, a refresh token), plus basic details about our own account: the account or page name, and for Pinterest, the account username and its list of boards. PostKraft uses these details only to confirm it is connected to the right account and to choose the board a Pin goes to.

PostKraft does not collect information about anyone else. It does not read other people's posts, Pins, boards, profiles, followers or messages, and it does not collect analytics.

2. How It Is Used

Tokens are used only to publish posts we have written and approved, at the time we scheduled them, to the account they belong to. For Pinterest, that means creating Pins on our own boards with our own photos, linking to articles on thekalmanco.com.

3. Where It Is Stored

Everything stays on that one computer. Tokens and app keys are encrypted with the macOS Keychain. Post drafts, our photos, and a log of what was published (the time, the platform and the ID of the post created) are kept in PostKraft's folder on that computer.

Instagram and Pinterest fetch images from a web address, so to publish a photo there PostKraft uploads that photo to Cloudinary, an image host, and gives the platform its address. Nothing else is sent anywhere except the post itself, to the platform it is published on.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share data from any platform. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not send it to AI services.

5. Keeping and Deleting Data

Tokens are kept until we disconnect the account in PostKraft, which deletes them from the computer. A connection can also be removed from the platform's side, in that platform's settings for apps with access to the account. The publishing log is kept as our record of what went out.

6. Third-Party Services

Each of these services has its own privacy policy for the data it holds:

  • Meta (Instagram, Facebook) — Meta Privacy Policy
  • LinkedIn — LinkedIn Privacy Policy
  • X — X Privacy Policy
  • Pinterest — Pinterest Privacy Policy
  • Cloudinary (image hosting for Instagram and Pinterest) — Cloudinary Privacy Policy

7. Children

PostKraft is not directed at children and collects nothing from them.

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the dates above.
