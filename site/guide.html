⚡ Quick start

Five minutes to your first post

If you only read one thing on this page, read this. Everything below is detail.

  1. Install the DMG from postkraft.app/download and drag PostKraft to your Applications folder.
  2. Sign up in the app with your email. Enter promo code CONFERENCE26 at checkout for your free month.
  3. Connect Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn in the onboarding wizard — three clicks each.
  4. Drop a listing photo into the Studio tab. PostKraft writes the caption, picks the format, and queues a post for each platform.
  5. Review and post. Tap publish or schedule for later. That's it.

1Install & sign in

Get the app on your Mac and create your account.

Download and install

  1. Go to postkraft.app/download on your Mac. The DMG will start downloading automatically.
  2. Open the downloaded PostKraft-1.0.1-arm64.dmg file.
  3. Drag the PostKraft icon to your Applications folder.
  4. Open PostKraft from Applications (or use Spotlight: ⌘ Space then type "PostKraft").
System requirements: Apple Silicon Mac (M1, M2, M3, or M4) running macOS 12 or later. A universal binary for Intel Macs is coming soon.

Create your account

  1. The first time you open PostKraft, you'll see a sign-up screen.
  2. Enter your email address and create a password.
  3. You'll be taken to Stripe checkout — enter promo code CONFERENCE26 for your free 30-day trial.
  4. Add your payment method. You won't be charged until day 31, and you'll lock in the $49/month founding rate forever.

2Set up your brand

A two-minute setup that makes every caption sound like you.

After sign-up, the onboarding wizard asks for a few essentials. The more you give it now, the better your first captions will be.

  • Company name: Your brokerage or personal brand (e.g., "Whitney Group NE").
  • Industry: Real Estate.
  • Bio: Two or three sentences about who you are and who you help. This becomes the foundation of every caption.
  • Brand voice: Pick the tone that fits how you actually communicate — friendly & approachable, professional & authoritative, bold & disruptive, etc.
You can edit this anytime from the Profile tab in the sidebar. Most people refine their brand voice after seeing a few posts come out.

3Connect your social accounts

Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn — three logins, three clicks each.

Instagram + Facebook

  1. Click Connect Meta in the onboarding wizard (or later in the Accounts tab).
  2. A browser window opens — log in with the Facebook account that manages your business pages.
  3. Select your Facebook Page and the connected Instagram Business account.
  4. Approve the permissions PostKraft needs to publish posts.
  5. You'll be returned to the app. You should see green check marks next to both.
Heads up: Instagram only allows third-party posting from Business or Creator accounts that are linked to a Facebook Page. If yours is set to Personal, switch it in the Instagram app first: Settings → Account → Switch to Professional Account.

LinkedIn

  1. Click Connect LinkedIn.
  2. Sign in with the account you post from.
  3. Approve the permissions and you're done.

PostKraft posts to your personal LinkedIn profile by default. Posting to LinkedIn Company Pages is on the roadmap.

4Fill in the Memory Bank

The single most important step. This is what makes PostKraft sound like you and not like every other AI.

The Memory Bank is a place to dump everything you know about your market, your neighborhood, and your favorite spots. PostKraft pulls from it every time it writes a caption. The richer it is, the more your posts read like they came from a local expert — because they did.

Three categories to start

  • Real Estate Market: Price tiers, days on market, what's selling, what's not, regulatory quirks (septic, flood zones, HOAs), local pitfalls buyers always miss.
  • Local Lifestyle: Your neighborhoods explained the way a local would. The light, the seasons, the community, what makes living here different.
  • Things To Do: Your actual go-to restaurants, the beach you send people to, the museum that's worth two hours, the bike route worth doing.

How to write it

Write like you're texting a friend who just moved to town. Specific. Personal. No marketing language. PostKraft will polish the tone for each platform — your job is to give it real substance.

Pro tip: Spend 20 minutes on this before your first post. You can keep adding to it forever, but those first 20 minutes are worth more than any other setup time.

Voice rules

At the bottom of each Memory Bank section, add a line about words to avoid and signature phrases you always use. PostKraft will follow these on every post.

Example: "Never say stunning, breathtaking, or luxury living. Always describe the property in concrete terms — square footage, bedrooms, what the kitchen actually looks like."

5Create your first post

Drop a photo. Get three drafts. Approve. Done.

  1. Open the Studio tab in the sidebar.
  2. Drag a listing photo (or several) into the upload area. JPG and PNG both work.
  3. Pick a theme: Market, Local Lifestyle, or Things To Do. This tells PostKraft what kind of post you want.
  4. Choose a tone — Conversational, Professional, Playful, Storytelling, or Educational.
  5. Click Generate. In about 10 seconds you'll see drafts for each connected platform: Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn.

What you get back

Each platform gets a draft formatted for its norms — Instagram leans visual and tight, LinkedIn goes longer and more professional, Facebook lands in the middle. PostKraft also suggests hashtags and a call-to-action.

Captions feel off? Click Regenerate for new variations, or edit directly in the preview. Every edit you make trains PostKraft on what you actually want.

6Schedule, edit, or publish

Post now, queue for later, or save as a draft.

Post immediately

Click Publish Now on any draft and PostKraft sends it to the platform. Should land within a few seconds.

Schedule for later

  1. Click the calendar icon on any draft.
  2. Pick a date and time. PostKraft will publish it automatically at that moment.
  3. View and manage scheduled posts in the Calendar tab.

Save as draft

Not ready to post? Click Save. It moves to the Library tab where you can come back and edit, schedule, or delete it later.

7Autopilot (optional, powerful)

Point PostKraft at a folder. Whenever you drop new listing photos in, drafts appear automatically.

  1. Open the Autopilot tab.
  2. Click Choose Folder and pick the folder where you save listing photos (e.g., ~/Pictures/Listings/).
  3. Set your weekly cadence per platform — for example, 3 Instagram, 2 Facebook, 2 LinkedIn posts per week.
  4. Toggle Autopilot on.

From there, every photo you drop in becomes a draft waiting for your approval. You stay in control of what publishes — Autopilot just removes the manual upload step.

8Manage your billing

Update payment methods, view invoices, cancel anytime.

  1. Open the Billing tab in the app.
  2. Click Manage Subscription.
  3. You'll be taken to the Stripe Customer Portal where you can update your card, download invoices, or cancel.

?Troubleshooting & common questions

The handful of issues that come up most often.

"My Instagram won't connect."

The Instagram account must be a Business or Creator account linked to a Facebook Page. Personal Instagram accounts can't post through any third-party tool — that's a Meta restriction. To switch: open the Instagram app on your phone → Settings → Account → Switch to Professional Account.

"The captions don't sound like me."

Fill in the Memory Bank with more detail and add Voice Rules at the bottom of each section. Then regenerate. PostKraft adapts to whatever you write in the Memory Bank, so the more specific you get, the more it sounds like you.

"A scheduled post didn't go out."

PostKraft needs to be running on your Mac to publish scheduled posts. If your Mac was asleep or the app was closed, the post will be marked as Failed in the Calendar tab. Click it to retry. Auto-publishing in the cloud (so your Mac doesn't need to be on) is on the roadmap.

"Can I post to TikTok / Pinterest / Twitter?"

Twitter/X is rolling out now. TikTok and Pinterest are coming in Phase 2. The full multi-platform support is the long-term direction.

"Can I post to multiple LinkedIn or Instagram accounts?"

Not yet — PostKraft is currently single-account per platform. Multi-account support is on the roadmap.

"How do I cancel?"

Billing tab → Manage Subscription → Cancel. You'll keep access through the end of your current billing period.
