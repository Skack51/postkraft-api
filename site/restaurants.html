How it works

From dish to posted
in under 60 seconds.

The hardest part of restaurant social media isn't the photo — it's finding the words. PostKraft handles that so you can focus on the food.

1

Drop your photo

Drag in a shot of tonight's special, a new dish, your dining room, or a behind-the-scenes moment. PostKraft works with any food photo.

2

PostKraft reads the image and your restaurant's voice, then generates a mouth-watering caption with relevant hashtags and a call to action. Done in seconds.

Review and approve

Tweak the wording if you want, or hit Approve. Nothing goes live without your sign-off — you stay in control without doing all the work.

PostKraft posts directly to Instagram, Facebook, and more — or schedules it for peak engagement time. Your followers see it. You didn't have to think about it.
