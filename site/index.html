PostKraft

PostKraft is the private publishing tool The Kalman Co. uses to post its own photography to its own social media accounts on Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X and Pinterest.

It runs on one computer at The Kalman Co. in Nantucket, Massachusetts. Every post is written ahead of time and approved by hand before it goes out. PostKraft has no sign-ups and is not offered to the public.
