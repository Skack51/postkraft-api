How it works

From photo to posted
in under 60 seconds.

PostKraft handles the part agents hate — staring at a blank caption field. You keep doing what you do best.

1

Drop your photo

Drag a listing photo, beach shot, or market update graphic into PostKraft. Or let Autopilot watch your camera roll automatically.

2

AI writes the caption

PostKraft reads the image, knows your brand voice and market, and generates a platform-ready caption with hashtags and a call to action. No emojis. No fluff.

3

Review and approve

Tweak the caption if you want, or hit Approve. You're always in control — nothing posts without your sign-off.

4

It goes out

PostKraft posts directly to Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn — or schedules it for peak time. You get the credit, PostKraft did the work.
